Delete Your SOlO Account

SOlO · Owned and operated by 1NE Inc.

This page explains how to request deletion of your SOlO account and the personal information associated with it. SOlO is a chronological social marketplace owned and operated by 1NE Inc., an Arizona corporation. You can delete your account from inside the app, or request deletion from this page without opening the app.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

In-app account deletion

  1. Open the SOlO app and sign in.
  2. Go to your account settings.
  3. Select the account-deletion option.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can’t access the app, or you have uninstalled it, you can ask us to delete your account by email.

  1. Send an email to privacy@soloapp.email from the email address associated with your SOlO account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Account.
  3. Include your SOlO username and a clear statement that you want your account and data deleted.

Email a deletion request

We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. You can also reach us through the in-app support and account-management functionality, or by writing to the Privacy Team at the address below.

What happens when your account is deleted

When an account is deleted, we delete or de-identify the personal information associated with it, unless retention is reasonably necessary and legally permitted. This includes your content: where deletion of User Content associated with your account is required, we delete it from active SOlO systems.

DataWhat happens
Account information (email address, username, credentials, account identifiers, eligibility confirmations)Deleted or de-identified
Profile information (name, photo, biography, links, portfolio, other profile fields)Deleted or de-identified
User Content (posts, photos, videos, listings, comments, advertisements, reviews)Deleted from active systems, subject to the exceptions below
Messages and communicationsDeleted or de-identified, subject to the exceptions below
Device and technical information, notification tokensDeleted or de-identified when no longer reasonably necessary

Information we may keep

We may retain certain information after deletion where retention is reasonably necessary and legally permitted, for purposes such as:

  • transaction and accounting records, including tax and accounting requirements;
  • fraud prevention;
  • security;
  • abuse prevention and enforcement records, including repeat-abuse prevention;
  • pending disputes and the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims;
  • regulatory requirements;
  • protection of other users; and
  • legal preservation obligations.

Content you shared with other users, such as messages they received, may remain available to them where permitted by law.

Backups

Deleted information may remain temporarily in backups until those backups are overwritten according to normal backup cycles. When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we seek to delete, de-identify, or securely dispose of it.

Paid features and subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel purchases or subscriptions made through Apple, Google Play, or another payment provider. If you have an active subscription, cancel it through that provider. Refunds are governed by the SOlO Payment Terms and the applicable app store’s rules.
